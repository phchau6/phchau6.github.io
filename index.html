Pak Hei, Jacky CHAU

I am a part-time Research Assistant (I) in the Department of Linguistics and Modern Language Studies at The Education University of Hong Kong, working with Dr. Xiaoyu Xu on an RGC General Research Fund project exploring the linguistic resources of museum texts preferred by diverse audiences.

Alongside this, I am a half-time Project Associate in the Educational Development Centre at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Dr. Julia Chen. I synthesise research on generative AI in education and prepare documents for management and external stakeholders. My earlier work at PolyU involved automated writing evaluation, adaptive learning, and online English language support.

I graduated from City University of Hong Kong with a BA in Linguistics and Language Applications with First Class Honours. Supervised by Dr. Lydia Catedral, my undergraduate dissertation examined newspapers’ portrayal of victims using critical discourse analysis and tactics of intersubjectivity.

My research interests lie at the intersection of language, accessibility, and education. I am particularly interested in:

  • Critical discourse analysis
  • Accessible museum communication
  • Generative AI in education
  • Technology-enhanced language learning
  • Learner agency

Based in Hong Kong

I welcome conversations about discourse analysis, accessible communication, and AI in language learning and education, including collaborations and related research opportunities. Feel free to get in touch.
